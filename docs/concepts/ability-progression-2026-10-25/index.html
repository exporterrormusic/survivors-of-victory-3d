A01 + A02 / A proposed replacement for the tree

Original five-rank grouping · superseded by the A02 revision

The starting abilities are free. Each later rank keeps earlier effects and adds a meaningful bundle. Former mastery effects become the final rank, so there is no separate “own every talent” requirement.

A01 / A02 — You choose the investment

Max Weapon early, spread points across the kit, or prioritize turrets. Each ability has a fixed sequence, but your build order remains yours.

A03 — Character level drives everything

No purchase decisions. Reaching the next milestone upgrades the whole kit automatically. The screen becomes a preview of what comes next.
