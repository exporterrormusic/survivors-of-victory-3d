GENRE SWITCH: DESIGN BRIEF FOR IMAGE MOCKUPS
NIKKE fan game (Babylon.js prototype)
Date: 2026-10-24

================================================================================
0. WHAT I NEED FROM YOU
================================================================================

This game is currently a MOBA. We are switching it to a SINGLE-PLAYER genre, because
building multiplayer is too much work. Three directions are under consideration (sections 3-5).
Please produce in-game screenshot mockups for each one, so we can compare them and choose.

- Make each image look like a real in-game screenshot, not concept art or a poster.
- Size: 16:9, 1920x1080.
- All three options should look like the same game. Keep the shared look in section 1
  and change only what the option needs.
- Each option has a numbered shot list. Make one image per shot. If you can only make a
  few, do shot A of each option first.
- After the images, write one short paragraph per option: what read well and what didn't.


================================================================================
1. THE SHARED LOOK (keep this in every mockup)
================================================================================

1.1 Characters
- The heroes are "Nikkes": the girls from SHIFT UP's game GODDESS OF VICTORY: NIKKE,
  drawn as 3D chibi models. The heads are big (roughly 1/3 of body height) and the
  bodies small, with anime faces and cel-ish shading. Their weapons are oversized
  compared with their bodies.
- At gameplay zoom a hero is about 1/12 of the screen tall. The hairstyle and the weapon
  are what identify each one.
- Playable heroes and their weapons:
    Snow White ..... silver-white hair, dark visor goggles, huge sniper/railgun
    Crown .......... blonde, blue eyes, small crown/tiara, rapid-fire minigun
    Rapunzel ....... blonde, rocket launcher carried sideways (explosive rockets)
    Scarlet ........ sword user (wide sweeping slashes)
    Marian ......... (Modernia form) continuous energy beam weapon
    Kilo ........... shotgun (5-pellet spread)
    Nayuta ......... a floating ring weapon that hovers behind her (no gun barrel)
    Sin ............ submachine guns
    Wells .......... sniper (uses the "Product 08" mass-produced Nikke body)
  If you don't know a character's exact look, use the hair and weapon above on a
  generic chibi soldier girl. Do not invent a different weapon.
- Rank-and-file allies are mass-produced chibi Nikkes: Soldier EG, Soldier FA,
  Soldier OW, Product 08, Product 12, Product 23, iDoll Flower, iDoll Ocean, iDoll Sun.
  They look uniform and military, and are less ornate than the heroes.

1.2 Enemies: Raptures
Raptures are mechanical alien machines, the series' main enemy. They are pale bone-white
and gunmetal plating with red glowing cores and eyes, and look insect-like or
walker-like. The game currently has three:
    Lure ...... small, fires single red bolts
    Pelican ... medium, bulky, fires a fan of bolts
    Crawler ... low and wide, fires arcing mortar shells
Raptures come in CROWDS. The engine draws hundreds on screen at once, and the mockups
should show that scale where the shot calls for it.

1.3 Camera
- High three-quarter overhead view, like League of Legends, Heroes of the Storm or
  Diablo. Pitch is about 55-60 degrees down, with a narrow field of view (about 40
  degrees), so perspective distortion is low.
- The player's hero is near screen center.

1.4 Environment art
- The style is hand-painted, like a painted miniature diorama: painterly textures on
  fairly simple geometry, with readable shapes and no photo-realism.
- Ground: large painted stone paving slabs with moss in the cracks and yellow dashed
  lane markings. Lush painted grass and woodland next to the paving, with ferns, broad
  leafy trees, mossy boulders and ruined concrete blocks.
- Military structures:
    - ivory armor panels, blue-gray recessed panels, ochre/yellow trim, vents, wear
    - twin-barrel gun towers
    - gate walls
    - blocky bunker-style Forts and Keeps
    - a Core with a mostly transparent energy shield dome
- Team energy color: player side BLUE/cyan glow, enemy side RED glow. It shows on
  towers, gates and light strips.
- Lighting: "painted daylight". A warm sun at a lowish angle gives soft directional
  shadows, there is a cool sky fill and ambient occlusion, and glow is used sparingly.
  Avoid washed-out bloom.

1.5 HUD (laid out like Heroes of the Storm, colored like NIKKE)
Colors:
    panel background  #111B27 at ~93% opacity (dark navy glass)
    panel edge        #8EA5B6 (cool steel)
    text              #F5F8FC
    muted text        #BDCBD6
    accent / player   #43C6F3 (cyan)
    headings / gold   #FFBD4C
    enemy             #EF6C69 (red)
Fonts: Rajdhani (or a similar squared sci-fi sans) for headings and numbers, and Segoe UI
style for body text. Headings are gold uppercase, for example "CHOOSE A HEROIC".
Frames are beveled chrome/steel with hexagon shapes throughout.

Current layout (keep it unless an option says otherwise):
- Bottom-left: a large hexagonal hero portrait in a chrome bevel. Next to it:
    - hero name
    - ammo pips (small yellow slashes plus a count, for example 7/7)
    - a GREEN health bar with numbers ("1450/1450")
    - a BLUE resource bar beneath it ("500/500")
- Bottom-center: a row of large hexagonal ability buttons Q W E R D. They have painted
  icons and metal bevel frames, and the key letter sits under each. Smaller
  parallelogram buttons sit at each end: Z (left) and B (right).
- Bottom-right: a square minimap in a dark navy frame, with blue and red structure
  icons. A thin music player strip sits on top of it: track name, previous, pause,
  next, mute.
- Top-center: currently the MOBA scoreboard. It has a blue/red score, a match timer,
  five portrait slots per team, and a "Core Health" strip under it. Each option below
  replaces this.
- Top-right: a small square hamburger menu button.
- Choice popups use the "heroic picker" look: a dark metal-framed tray, a gold
  uppercase heading, cards with beveled borders, hexagonal icon sockets, number-key
  badges (1, 2, 3) in the corners, and cyan hover states.

1.6 In-world UI rules (strict)
- ONLY heroes get a name above their head. Minions, Raptures, summons and buildings get
  health bars only, with no name labels.
- Above a hero: the name, a segmented green health bar, and a thin blue resource bar
  under it, high enough to clear the head.
- A cyan selection ring sits under the player's hero, and enemies being targeted get a
  red ring.
- NO status text anywhere in the world: no "STUNNED", "SLOWED", "HASTE" and so on.
  Show statuses as effects on the model instead:
    stun = spinning sparks or electric crackle above the head
    slow = icy blue frost
    burn = small flames
    shield = translucent bubble
    haste = speed streaks
    vulnerable = red target-mark glow
- Ability telegraphs are flat decals on the ground:
    line skillshot = a long translucent rectangle
    area = a circle
    cone = a wedge
  Cyan means the player's, red means the enemy's.

2. RULES FOR ALL THREE OPTIONS
================================================================================

- Single-player only. Show no other human players and no 5v5 team bars. "Allies" are AI.
- Keep the hero ability bar (Q W E R D, health and resource). Each hero's kit carries
  over unchanged.
- Keep the painted environment, structure style and Rapture enemies.
- The Survivors-style front end (main menu, Character Select, Stage Select, Shop,
  Records, Achievements) still exists. Mock up a menu screen only if a shot asks for one.

3. OPTION 1: "HOLD THE ARK" (hero defense)
================================================================================

Pitch
  The Ark is humanity's last city. Raptures push down the lanes in waves of hundreds,
  and you defend the gates, towers and Core with one Nikke hero, supported by a thin
  line of mass-produced Nikke troops and turrets you place. The lane maps and
  structures we already have stay. The player is simply always the defending side.

Loop
  Wave phase: fight the Raptures pushing down one to three lanes, using your abilities
  and your towers.
  Build phase (about 30 seconds between waves): place or upgrade turrets on build pads,
  repair gates, spend credits in the Shop, and pick a talent.
  Every few waves a boss Rapture leads the wave.
  You win by surviving the final wave. You lose if the Core is destroyed.

HUD changes
- Top-center: replace the scoreboard with a wave panel showing "WAVE 7 / 20", a
  countdown to the next wave during the build phase, and the Core health strip
  underneath (one bar, ours only).
- Next to the wave panel, a credits counter (gold coin-like icon plus a number).
- Build phase only: a build bar above the ability bar, with four or five turret cards
  in the heroic-picker style. Each card shows an icon, a credit cost and a hotkey.
- Minimap: red chevrons show which lanes the next wave enters.

Shot list
  1A  WAVE DEFENSE AT THE GATE
      A stone lane runs from upper right to lower left, toward a blue-lit Fort gate
      flanked by two twin-barrel towers firing blue tracers. A crowd of 150+ Raptures
      (mostly Lures, some Pelicans and Crawlers) packs the lane, with red mortar arcs
      overhead. Snow White stands just in front of the gate firing a long cyan
      line-skillshot that pierces a column of Raptures. A burn zone smolders on the
      ground. Four or five mass-produced Nikke troops hold the line beside her. The HUD
      shows "WAVE 7 / 20" top-center and the Core strip at full health. The mood is
      frantic but readable.

  1B  BUILD PHASE
      The same area, calm, with wreckage of the last wave scattered on the paving. A
      ghost turret (translucent cyan) hovers over a hexagonal build pad, with a cyan
      range ring on the ground. Two turrets already placed sit on nearby pads. The build
      bar shows turret cards with costs. Top-center reads "NEXT WAVE IN 0:24", and the
      credits show 1,250. Crown idles near the pads.

  1C  BOSS WAVE BREACH
      A huge Rapture boss (several times a Pelican's size: bone-white plating, glowing
      red core, insectoid legs) is smashing through a destroyed gate, with rubble and
      sparks around it. A long boss health bar runs across the top-center under the
      wave panel, with the boss name in gold uppercase. The Core's shield dome is
      visible in the background. Rapunzel is firing rockets while backing up, and red
      danger circles on the ground mark incoming mortar impacts.

  1D  MAP OVERVIEW (optional)
      A zoomed-out tactical view of a three-lane map. The Ark base is on the left, blue
      glowing. The Rapture spawn on the right is red and organic-mechanical. Wave arrows
      show red streams entering two lanes. This could serve as a pre-wave camera or a
      Stage Select image.

4. OPTION 2: "SURFACE RUN" (action roguelite, Hades/Diablo-like)
================================================================================

Pitch
  Each run is one Nikke's expedition onto the Rapture-held Surface. You fight through
  a chain of arena rooms, pick an upgrade after each room, face a boss at the end of
  each sector, and when you die you return to the Ark to spend permanent upgrades. The
  hero kits become the starting build, and each hero's talent tree (already written)
  becomes the pool of upgrades offered during a run.

Loop
  Enter a room. Rapture crowds spawn in waves. Clear it, then choose one of three
  rewards: a talent, a heroic, credits or a heal. Choose an exit door (each door shows
  the reward behind it). A boss waits at the end of each sector. The Shop in the Ark
  hub sells permanent upgrades between runs.

Look
  The rooms are enclosed arenas built from the same painted assets: ruined concrete,
  cliffs, overgrown paving and woodland, plus military wreckage. The areas are smaller
  and more enclosed than the lanes, with walls and cover inside the room. Sectors vary
  in lighting: painted daylight, warm dusk, cool woodland.

HUD changes
- Top-center: a run progress strip replaces the scoreboard. It shows the sector name in
  gold uppercase (for example "SECTOR 2: OVERGROWN OUTPOST") and a row of small room
  pips. Cleared pips are cyan, the current one is outlined, and the boss is a red
  skull-hex at the end.
- Top-left: the run's collected upgrades as a row of small hex icons.
- Credits counter near the top-center.
- The minimap is optional, either smaller or removed, since rooms are small.

Shot list
  2A  ARENA COMBAT
      An enclosed ruined plaza with broken pillars and moss. Scarlet is center-screen
      mid-slash: a wide cyan cone telegraph on the ground and a bright sword arc. A ring
      of 40-60 Raptures closes in from all sides. Several Raptures in front of her are
      knocked back, and a few show frost (slow) and flames (burn). Pelicans in the back
      show red fan telegraphs. The collected upgrade icons run along the top-left.

  2B  REWARD PICK
      The cleared room is dimmed behind a centered heroic-picker-style tray headed
      "CHOOSE A TALENT", holding three tall cards. Each card has a hex icon socket, a
      talent name, a tier label, a short description and a 1/2/3 badge. Example card:
      "Weak Point: Bullet hits mark the target; it takes extra damage from all sources."
      One card is in the cyan hover state.

  2C  BOSS ROOM
      A large circular arena. A giant Rapture boss is in the upper half, and the floor
      is covered in red telegraph patterns (expanding rings, radial lines, mortar
      circles) that the player has to dodge. Marian fires her continuous cyan beam
      into the boss. The boss health bar spans the top-center. Shell casings and debris
      are scattered around.

  2D  EXIT DOORS
      The far wall of a cleared room has two or three exit gateways (military blast
      doors set in ruins). Each has a glowing hex reward icon floating above it: talent,
      credits, heal, heroic. Kilo walks toward them.

5. OPTION 3: "SQUAD OF FIVE" (real-time squad tactics with pause)
================================================================================

Pitch
  This is closest to how NIKKE itself plays. You command a squad of five Nikkes on
  missions across the Surface, using cover and timing their Burst skills in a chain:
  Burst I, then Burst II, then Burst III, ending in a FULL BURST damage window. The
  play is real-time, but you can slow or pause time to give orders. The same
  click-to-move controls and cover system already exist. Allied Nikkes are AI-driven
  when you aren't directly controlling them.

Loop
  Pick a mission and a five-Nikke squad. In the field: move the squad between cover,
  focus targets and use abilities. A squad Burst gauge fills as you deal damage, and
  when it's full you chain Burst I, II and III for a Full Burst. Mission types are
  hold, escort, clear and extract. Raptures attack in crowds from several directions.

HUD changes
- Bottom-left: the single portrait becomes FIVE smaller hexagonal squad portraits in a
  row. Each has its own health and resource bars, a small Burst tier badge (I, II or
  III), and a dimmed overlay when that Nikke's burst is on cooldown. The selected
  Nikkes get a cyan outline.
- Bottom-center: the ability bar shows the selected Nikke's Q W E, slightly smaller.
- Above the ability bar sits the squad Burst gauge, a wide segmented bar with three
  chain slots labelled I, II and III. Slots light up gold as the chain progresses.
- Top-left: an objective tracker in a navy panel, for example "HOLD THE RELAY 1:42" or
  "EXTRACT: 3/5 ALIVE".
- Top-center: the mission timer. There is no scoreboard.
- Minimap stays bottom-right.

In-world additions
- Cover markers: small shield icons on cover objects. Full cover is a solid icon and
  half cover is a half icon. They appear when a unit is selected.
- Move-order markers on the ground: cyan chevrons or rings, with dotted paths from
  each Nikke.
- Every squad member gets a name over the head, because they are all heroes.

Shot list
  3A  SQUAD IN COVER
      Five Nikkes (for example Snow White, Crown, Rapunzel, Kilo and Nayuta) spread
      behind ruined concrete barricades and a wrecked vehicle, with cover shield icons
      visible. Two are selected (cyan rings), and a cyan move-order marker with dotted
      paths leads to a forward position. A large mixed Rapture group advances across an
      open overgrown plaza toward them, with incoming red bolts. The objective tracker
      sits top-left.

  3B  FULL BURST
      The climax. The Burst gauge's three slots (I, II, III) are all lit gold, and a
      "FULL BURST" banner in gold uppercase appears with a brief screen-edge flash. All
      five Nikkes fire at once: the sniper line, the minigun stream, rocket explosions,
      the shotgun spread, Nayuta's ring glowing. A dense Rapture cluster takes the hits.
      The image should feel cinematic, but the HUD must stay visible.

  3C  TACTICAL PAUSE
      The same battlefield, desaturated with a cool blue tint, with "PAUSED" or a slow-mo
      indicator top-center. Queued orders are drawn as numbered dotted paths and ability
      previews, such as a cyan circle where Rapunzel's rockets will land and a line
      where Snow White will fire. Enemy intent appears as faint red arrows.

  3D  SQUAD SELECT (optional, front-end menu)
      A menu screen with five empty hex slots across the top and a grid of nine Nikke
      portraits below. Each portrait has a Burst tier badge (I/II/III) and a weapon
      icon. A warning appears if the squad lacks a Burst I, II or III. Use the dark navy
      and gold palette.

================================================================================
7. DON'T
================================================================================

- Don't make realistic or non-chibi characters.
- Don't add other human players, chat boxes, a K/D scoreboard or 5v5 team bars.
- Don't put status words over units.
- Don't use neon cyberpunk colors or purple-heavy palettes. Stay with navy, steel, cyan,
  gold and red, on painted natural greens and stone.
- Don't draw tiny or cluttered UI. It should read big and clear, like Heroes of the
  Storm.
